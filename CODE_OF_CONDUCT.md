# Code of Conduct

Memora is a small open-source project. Participation requires respectful,
professional behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy: never publish another person's data, credentials or private
  conversations.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and architecture gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, spam or bad-faith security testing.
- Using the project to build or distribute malware or to violate applicable law.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments or issues that violate
this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
